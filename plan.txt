About Us
Our Story
We all started from somewhere, some in the break room of their mother’s office job,
some of us slowly started sipping out of Daddy’s morning cup, or maybe you’re like me,
American… and ran on Dunkin.
What started as the perfect part-time job to make some money before college turned out to be a
part of a much bigger picture. Working at Dunkin gave me a chance to create, which is something
I’ve always been good at. Whether it was magnets glued to leftover rolls of wrapping paper (a
magnet vacuum of course, very useful), or as simple as a latte.
As a young barista who was on the other side of the counter most of her life, I’ve always had the
privilege of knowing what a customer would be interested in. Before strawberry matcha lattes were
on every menu in America, it was on Dunkin’s stand-up chalkboard as “The Sophia.” (Creative,
right?)
Every week customers looked forward to seeing what new drink I’d create. It was impossible not to
notice the joy a simple latte could bring.
Once college came, Dunkin was left behind—but lattes never were.
College is exciting, but it also comes with two major necessities: money and caffeine. Between
classes, sports, studying, and trying to have a social life, coffee quickly becomes part of your daily
routine. Before long, everyone is grabbing dining hall coffee just hoping to survive Statistics class.
But after buying food, joining clubs, and paying for everything else college throws at you, there isn’t
much money left.
That’s when Sophia’s Lattes was born.
With a background in crafting everything from medium hot regulars to caramel macchiatos, I started
selling handcrafted lattes right out of my dorm room. What began as a small side hustle quickly
became a familiar name across campus.
As our name grew, so did we.
Selling lattes on the mountain was an unforgettable experience—but eventually, everyone comes
home.
And where better to continue the journey than New York?

Mission Statement
Our mission as a small business is to provide simple, affordable, and reliable lattes for our
customers.
Starting small has never been a limitation. We believe that by putting our customers first,
consistently introducing exciting new menu items, and staying ahead of New York’s coffee trends
while respecting the timeless art of coffee, we can build a brand people genuinely love.
Sophia’s Lattes is proudly college student-owned, and we sincerely appreciate every customer and
every charitable donation that helps support our journey.
