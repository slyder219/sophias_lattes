Our Story

We all started from somewhere. Some in the break room of a parent's office job, some sipping from Dad's morning cup, and some of us running on Dunkin. What started as a part-time job before college became part of a much bigger picture.

Working at Dunkin opened a space to create. Before strawberry matcha lattes were on every menu, it showed up on the stand-up chalkboard as "The Sophia." Customers came back every week to see what was new, and it was impossible not to notice how much joy a simple latte could bring.

Once college came, Dunkin was left behind, but lattes never were. Between classes, sports, studying, and social life, coffee became part of everyone's routine. With college budgets stretched thin, Sophia's Lattes was born.

What started as handcrafted lattes from a dorm room quickly became a familiar name across campus. As the name grew, so did the business. The mountain chapter was unforgettable, and now the journey continues in New York.

Mission Statement

Our mission is to provide simple, affordable, and reliable lattes. Starting small has never been a limitation. By putting customers first, introducing exciting menu items, and honoring timeless coffee craft while keeping pace with New York trends, we aim to build a brand people genuinely love.
